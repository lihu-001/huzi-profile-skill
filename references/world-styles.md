# World style catalog (50)

Selectable graphic-design languages. Not a second brand. Pick one number after the IP sentence exists.

When the user names a number, a Chinese/English style name, or says 全部 / all styles / 对照, use this file.

## How to run

1. Manufacture 1500x500 canvas, then `render_edited_image`. Never use landscape generate as the final banner.
2. Carry the selected design language across the full canvas. Keep type and key focal objects outside the bottom-left avatar circle; backgrounds and non-critical style elements may continue through it.
3. Wordless ground, type in a later pass, contrast per SKILL.md steps 6–7 and references/design-bar.md.
4. Never put adjectives, percents, or prompt notes next to the brand name.
5. No musicians, instruments, or a second persona unless that is the IP.
6. One style = one design language. Cover the name — the method must still be visible.

Catalog mode (user asks for all, 对照, 选风格)

- Draw banners in batches of 10 on the same 3-1 canvas.
- Label each image with its number and name in the chat, not on the picture.
- Do not also emit 50 avatars unless asked.
- After a pick, derive the avatar from that language only.

## Catalog

### 01 Swiss
Grid, flush grotesque, objective. Prompt — faint modular grid, flush-left wordmark, one banner line.

### 02 Bauhaus
Circle bar square, primary color, form follows function.

### 03 Constructivism
Diagonal bars, industrial grain, block type as a vector.

### 04 De Stijl
Only horizontals and verticals, red yellow blue rectangles.

### 05 New Typography
Asymmetric rules, Akzidenz-like grotesque, no centered axis.

### 06 Art Nouveau
Whiplash plant line, tall Latin, olive gold. No Victorian dump, no orchestra.

### 07 Art Deco
Chevron, sunburst, condensed gold on black.

### 08 Arts and Crafts
Woodcut botanical border, honest roman on cream. Pattern as ethics, not a folk band.

### 09 Vienna Secession
Gold square grid, Hoffmann geometry, black field.

### 10 Futurism
Diagonal speed, slashing type. Not a sports-game trailer.

### 11 Dada
Torn newsprint, mixed wood type, stamped line askew.

### 12 Suprematism
One black square, one red rectangle, tiny type.

### 13 Sachplakat
One object on one flat field. Object is the poster.

### 14 Streamline Moderne
Chrome curve, speed lines, rounded sans. Machine, not a jazz club.

### 15 Heroic Realism
Monumental flat silhouette + caption. Graphic, not a portrait.

### 16 Corporate Modern
One witty geometric mark beside a large simple wordmark.

### 17 Mid-century Modern
Organic kidney shape, ochre and teal, friendly sans. Print ad, not furniture or horns.

### 18 Ulm
Cool gray modules, label + spec line. No decoration.

### 19 Crouwel
Tight tracking, number-grid ghost, monospaced banner line.

### 20 Lubalin
Tight phototype, geometric grotesque. Letters almost touch, every letter complete. Prompt — huge lockup, wide-tracked banner line, no grid.

### 21 Pop Art
Ben-Day dots, comic lettering. No stage kit.

### 22 Psychedelic
Molten letterforms only. No concert photo.

### 23 Push Pin
Flat illustrative pastiche shapes. No people.

### 24 Polish Poster
Painterly metaphor, dusk limited palette.

### 25 Japanese Modern
Huge red disk, Latin wordmark. Kamekura force.

### 26 Wood type
Hamilton-poster letters, oversized and complete, slight two-color misregistration. Not a cropped supergraphic.

### 27 Punk DIY
Xerox, stencil, torn paper.

### 28 New Wave
Broken Swiss grid, stepped type, rules at wrong angles.

### 29 Memphis
Squiggle, terrazzo, hot pink lime. Pattern clash, no dancers.

### 30 Deconstructivism
Fractured layered wordmark, colliding columns.

### 31 Emigre
Coarse bitmap display, lo-res second line.

### 32 Grunge
Overprint, scratch, photocopier dirt.

### 33 Maximalism
Dense pattern, wordmark still readable. No synth wall.

### 34 Brutalism
Type cast into concrete, plaque line.

### 35 Wabi-sabi
Stained handmade sheet, imperfect ink, torn edge.

### 36 Bass
Cut-paper planes, condensed complete title type. One or two angular color fields. Not a portrait, not a film still.

### 37 Superflat
Even color planes, no depth, no mascot.

### 38 Op Art
Black-white vibrating field, type interrupts it. No figures.

### 39 Concrete
Squares as proportion. Bill, not a toy band.

### 40 Agitprop
Bold italic word in a red bar on black. Full words only. No pictogram, no tiny legend.

### 41 Flat Design
Solid UI blocks, no shadow.

### 42 Material Design
Overlapping colored sheets, soft paper shadow.

### 43 Skeuomorphism
Embossed plate, physical material. Still 3-1.

### 44 Glassmorphism
Frosted panel, sharp type on glass, readable.

### 45 Neumorphism
Same-color soft extrusion on pale gray.

### 46 Y2K
Chrome bubble type, icy highlight. Readable.

### 47 Synthwave
Purple-cyan grid horizon, type on the horizon. Not a wallpaper dump.

### 48 Acid Graphics
Toxic melt gradient, distorted but readable wordmark.

### 49 Neo-brutalism
System font, thick border, alignment marks.

### 50 Risograph
Fluorescent two-color overprint, grainy stencil, complete oversized word. Pink plus teal, slight misregistration. Not a neon sign.

## Prompt skeleton

Keep this exact 1500 by 500 three-to-one canvas. Carry [style language] across the full width; do not leave a generic blank left third. Wordless ground — no readable words, no brand name. Keep key focal objects outside the bottom-left avatar circle and mobile crop edges. No people unless the IP is a face. Set the banner line in a later pass; it must contrast the local field (references/design-bar.md).

## Pairing with studio textures

references/style-packs.md is a shorter recipe book for one-person-company jobs. If the user picks a world-style number, that number wins. Do not mix Swiss + Memphis on one account.
